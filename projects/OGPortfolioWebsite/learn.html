Consider Bullet Points

9/5/2025

Are you considering desinging your own portfolio website like this one?

Bullet points may be the way to go when discussing projects.
• Bullet points are quicker for site visitors to read.
• They make it quicker and easier to post new information because you do not need to write entire paragraphs.
• They are commonly used on a resume meaning that the bullet points used describing projects can easily be modified to work in a resume too.
• After learning this while writing my first resume, I am currently in the process of transitioning some of my paragraphs to bullet points on my website.

• Remember, your website is your own, so choosing when and how to use bullet points is an important "point" to consider when listing information.
